## 📖 Description

**Elemental Destiny** is an enchanting dice game inspired by **Yahtzee**. Each roll summons one of six elemental forces, and players must use strategy and luck to achieve the highest score possible.

Players roll **5 dice**, each with 6 possible faces:

| Face | Element | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | 🔥 Fire | Aggressiveness and power |
| 2 | 💧 Water | Adaptability and revival |
| 3 | 🌍 Earth | Shelter and security |
| 4 | 💨 Air | Speed and quickness |
| 5 | ⚡ Thunder | Immediate effect |
| 6 | ✨ Wild | Can represent any element for scoring |

Each turn consists of:

1. An initial roll of all 5 dice
2. Up to **2 re-rolls**
3. Choosing which dice to hold before each re-roll
4. Selecting **one unused scoring category**

The game continues until all required categories have been filled.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### 1. Roll the Dice

At the beginning of each turn, roll all five dice.

### 2. Hold and Re-roll

You may re-roll up to **two times**.

Before each re-roll, choose which dice you want to **hold**. Held dice will remain unchanged while the remaining dice are rolled again.

You may also choose to stop rolling early.

### 3. Choose a Scoring Category

After your final roll, select **one scoring category that has not already been used**.

Your score is calculated according to the rules of that category.

> **Note:** Once a category has been used, it cannot be selected again.

### 4. Continue Playing

Continue taking turns until all required scoring categories have been completed.

A score sheet is displayed after each round.

# 🧙 Elements

### 🔥 Fire
Represents aggressiveness and power.

### 💧 Water
Represents adaptability and revival.

### 🌍 Earth
Represents shelter and security.

### 💨 Air
Represents speed and quickness.

### ⚡ Thunder
Represents immediate effect.

### ✨ Wild
Can be converted into another element for scoring.

**Wild Rule:**

- Only **one Wild conversion** is allowed per roll.
- Some categories do not allow Wild conversion.
